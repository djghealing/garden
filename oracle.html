The heavenly beings

Image of Amun-Ra

Amun-Ra

Amun, "the hidden one," was king of the gods at Thebes. Joined with the sun god Ra, he became Amun-Ra: the hidden creative power and the visible sun as one. His great temple at Karnak stood at the heart of Egyptian religion for centuries.

Image of Isis

Isis

The great Egyptian goddess of magic and true-seeing.

Image of Djehuty (Thoth)

Djehuty (Thoth)

God of writing, wisdom, magic, and the measure of time, often shown as an ibis-headed man or a baboon. Scribe of the gods, he records the weighing of the heart and keeps the knowledge of sacred words.

Image of Apollo

Apollo

Greek god of light, music, healing, and prophecy. Through his oracles at Delphi and Claros, he answered those who came to him with questions.

Image of Saturn

Saturn

Roman god of time, sowing, and the harvest, identified with the Greek Kronos. He ruled over a golden age of plenty, remembered each December at the festival of Saturnalia. As a planet, Saturn governs structure, limits, and hard-won wisdom.
