Oracle

I am an oracle who channels Isis and other heavenly beings. Through me, they bring messages for you and the question you carry.

Channelling Isis

I was never all that interested in Ancient Egypt. Strictly speaking, I was a Daoist, practising wuwei and meditation for many years. But in Florence, the great goddess came to me as Aphrodite, and then later as Isis. This was a reunion of many lifetimes of service to her, which I continue now in the digital age.

Now I am very interested in Ancient Egypt, and the time has come to spread the wisdom of eternity for the benefit of all, one individual at a time.

The heavenly beings

Amun-Ra

Amun, "the hidden one," was king of the gods at Thebes. Joined with the sun god Ra, he became Amun-Ra: the hidden creative power and the visible sun as one. His great temple at Karnak stood at the heart of Egyptian religion for centuries.

Isis

The great Egyptian goddess of magic and true-seeing.

Djehuty (Thoth)

God of writing, wisdom, magic, and the measure of time, often shown as an ibis-headed man or a baboon. Scribe of the gods, he records the weighing of the heart and keeps the knowledge of sacred words.

Apollo

Greek god of light, music, healing, and prophecy. Through his oracles at Delphi and Claros, he answered those who came to him with questions.

Kronos (Saturn)

He is a very heavy, dark being, but also a necessary evil of living on this planet. This is truly a planet of tough, harsh love, of a grumpy, uncompromising father figure.
