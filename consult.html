Syncretic confession

Confession is a spiritual technology, and a very effective one. Speaking aloud what weighs on you, before a witness, loosens its hold, leaving you lightened.

It is also ancient. In Egypt, the dead stood in the Hall of the Two Truths and declared their innocence before forty-two divine judges while their heart was weighed against the feather of Maat, as set out in the Book of the Dead. The living confessed too: workmen at Deir el-Medina carved stelae admitting their wrongs to Ptah and Meretseger, and asking for mercy. In a past life I served Meretseger, and she was the first Egyptian goddess to show herself to me, hence her centrality on this website.

This offering is non-denominational, as all beings hold weight, and held without judgement: an hour to talk about anything at all, and to speak freely about whatever you need to set down before a non-judgemental witness.
