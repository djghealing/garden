Practices/pseudo‑tantras

Traditionally, all tantras come from forms of either Shiva or Shakti. These rituals came from Isis. The word is used here only because there is no clear English equivalent for the use of spells, meaning, and visualisation in ritual to develop the qualities of divinity.

The Practice of Geb

Dirtied feet, holder of trees,
Geb you are one I would like to meet

Be with me, support me true
I would like to share my weights amongst you

[Said full, with effervescence, trust, and restful spirit]:

All disconnects of my earth and all about
Fade to the earth

All malice of my earth of my passions misplaced
Fade to the earth

All alienation found and my views there around
Fade to the earth

All unclarity thereby
Fade to the earth

My unwillingness laid by
Fade to the earth

All aversions of the pasts
Fade to the earth

All carried-still defeats
Fade to the earth

All the ways I constrict
Fade to the earth

All bubbling-pains then
Fade to the earth

All my deepest of shames
Fade to the earth

And my clattering doubts
Fade to the earth

Still-resounding grief
Fade to the earth

And my sickness of spirit
And my lack of charm
And my regrets and whimpers
And my lacks and failures
All of it drifts
To you to share
My earth is ours
My service fair
Of this earth I gain
And to you this is owed
Carry this right
And soon my earth returns.

The Practice of Khepri

Khepri

worlds away
sized as all
beetle rolls means of your life
looking forward, burning heat
for you he turns once again
beetle armed with mighty soul

TEN AH KEPRI AH
ATAH AH KEPRI AH
TEN BOH KEPRI NEHEH BAH
AH AH KEPRI AH

thin arms can move more
with ra power small and yet not sore
thin arms can true move true
by ra power so can you

visualise oneself dissolving into a beetle the size of a grain of sand
visualise this beetle growing into infinite size as the sun

with this heart and spirit
do move the sun
being ra with you
Khepri has won
