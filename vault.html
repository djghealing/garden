Practices/Pseudo‑tantras

Traditionally, all tantras come from forms of either Shiva or Shakti. These practices came from Isis. The word is used here only because there is no clear English equivalent for the use of spells, meaning, and visualisation in ritual to develop the qualities of divinity.

Rituals

The Procedures to Honour the Great Mother Isis

[Before an image of Isis, Ma'at, Mut, Nut, Seshat, Hathor, or a small bird]

Glory to the ones who have passed and stayed!
Glory to this nature of infinite forms!
Glory to the eternal mother of magic who cares for us!
I praise her goodness and mercy!
Will she bless me as I bless her?

To the mother as the primordial waters, Mut, I offer water. (Water offering)

To the mother as love in Hathor, I offer natural sound. (Sound offering)

HAH RE AH SI NEH HEH ROH MAHDI x 70

To the mother of magic in Isis, I stand with still-movement to honour your ka.

Stand in an Anjali Mudra-like hand posture above the head for 7 minutes. (Physical offering: deity yoga visualisation of Isis's throne in the crown.)

Closing Promise

To the Great Goddess of my heart:

I will take time to honour you, infinite eternal lady, of nature, love, and mercy. Forgive me for where I have not been as you; teach me to grow bold, brave, open, and true. Allow me to live gracefully, with beauty and poise, to see the Good, to know the Good, to pursue the balance of your heavens and your earth. I will never dominate the feminine, only honour it.

Glory to your Nature in me! I carry you with me! You carry me with you!

Devotional Poems

Rest Bringer

Vesta, Oh Vesta,
of Sacred Rest.
Bless my home
from North
to West.
Let family proudly
bear our crest
And thrive today in
pleasant nest.

Aphrodite

The one who loves
the one who covers
she needs me to be me.
The love that shimmers
the one who catches
she that warms me.
I cannot leave
without her eyes
her curves to launch by.
Her sunny gaze
has my whole to raise
eyes closed
with my open heart.
I don't want her to leave
as she is the world
and without her
life is bleak.
but we are each
and each are we
and when she might be gone -
she's there.

The door is open.
