How I heal

Ancestral methods, remedying conceptual obscurations, clearing cords and entities, as well as balancing and realigning chakras and elemental flow in the body, spirit, and mind: all aimed at the highest timelines and the fullest expression of your natural qualities.

All healing is done intuitively in wuwei, from expanded awareness and natural "don't know" wisdom mind.

What to expect

During healing sessions, I often receive spiritual guidance and information specifically for you. If this is what you want, I recommend you check out my Oracle page.

After

We take a few minutes to land and talk about anything that came up, and you're welcome to follow up with any questions afterwards. Drink water and go gently for the rest of the day.
