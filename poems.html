Swallowed Alive

When met, your eye
a shaken hush
a blackening rush
through that we touch
where I'm inside
in sea walls shied
I wink a spy
light's dark abounds
I rest and am blessed
in my surrounds.

give, give, give of all

give, give, give of all
rosaries count big and small

the one who pours out ever beginning
that one who blocks so deepens sinning

you give, to give, generous then true
you pour out, to pour out, so-ever renewed

as all who give reward the world
so celebrate with sounds sweet swirled

an ocean outsoaks returned in rain
jealous sands that dried recede in pain

and yet - unfree care is forced, not giving
an apple tree gives by settling, so maintains a living

to give big, give small, maintain your scope
more you give, great flame, the greatest smoke.

Fair Notice

wrapped, then, in ruby net
blushing film over top
shimmering, shaking, dwelling now
I'd hoped, then, you'd not forget.

drive to death a forward shark
turning back, turning not
want to turn, but you forget
the shroud of end, do not beget.

swum in death, swum in death
I'd hoped, then, you'd not forget
of tripped pain, know no mend
yet now, you aim, aim black, but yet.

learn not from others, you go pretend
that you're not terrified of the end
warnings slide, you're safe, for mend
warned fools drown, do not pretend.

Fastened

Untipped unpoured
love’s unsprint unlured
The quorum locked-need
of Second Key to feed
Freeing shackles thus
‘Tis not a rush, ‘tis not a rush,
Open shackles only when
when only time is right - friend
And if ‘twill never be again,
then it will never be again or again and again.
And now of now and now and now?
I pray for keys jangling, for the love of dao.

Time Taken By

A spiral wind has taken me
All around within
Absolute confidence abounds for thee
This is to be a win
But then your boots go sink and set
Somewhere else to go
But time and weight shall not forget
As what you are you know
So as things go more and more
As salt waste makes elves cry
Consider the bounding leaps as sure
A world is not for options by

Forever

two mirrors gleam at each other
and shatter into sprinkles
they gazed through on with another
glistened and glowed too much for each other
primordial light won't handle itself
nothing else please, and everything self
brighter than brighter and yet nothing else
mirrors gleam won't handle itself

Neti, Neti

neti, neti
pot in nose
discard all concepts
life bloom grows

neti, neti
the hook goes deep
brain is discarded
not needed to leap

neti, neti
all weights are empty
without gravity, then
on tar sprint plenty

neti, neti
all pain drifts by
not this, not that
all words are lies

neti, neti
will you trade your view
a tumble of word sets
for a life renewed?

Grass and Scree

though you never got to see
me as me, we as we
assure I do that it is sweet
that on the day we surely meet
that I will go to lively greet
and me as me can still be there,

dead dreams will blink to leap awake
and me as we, we as we
'twas always there in layers deep
that is all that fate did see
a torrid path we walked did we
those choices made with rocks and scree
gave mountain shade and grass and glee.

Let the great act continue

Can a candle know its limits before burn’s end, can a sow share what is known from yesteryear’s rend?

When a fool lives in light, stumbling blind and without spite, will those that can see still make sense of his plight?

Every lesson dies before it can show itself known, every impression lies as though its care was not shown.

Memory is lies in the best of days but when diminished it can’t learn to even crest its own waves.

What am I then, doomed to submerge, when standards afloat, are my weighted calls heard?

When will she see that I am not as she, and that replacing oneself is not the principle she has seen?

And am I then useless, tossed between, an arrogant coin, in the couch of a queen?
