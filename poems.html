Poems

Some of what comes through doesn't fit in a session. It lands here.

Threshold

You do not have to knock.
The door was never locked,
only heavy with the years
you spent believing
you had to earn the room.
Come in. Set it down.
The floor will hold you.

After a session

Something in you unclenched
and did not ask permission.
Let it. Drink water.
Walk home the long way.
The light you felt was not mine.
I only held the lamp
while you remembered where you kept it.

Offering

Salt for the threshold,
rose for the heart,
smoke for whatever
is ready to go.
And for what stays,
your own two hands,
open.
